# Consortium Evidence Governance Policy — v3.0 (scheduled)

Announced ahead of time: this version is anchored now but declared to take
effect at a later date, so participants can prepare for the change before it
governs any submission.

## Changes relative to v2.0

C1 Conservation tolerance is tightened further, from 0.25 to 0.10 units.
C2 All other clauses of v2.0 remain in force unchanged.

## Status

This version is used in the prototype to demonstrate two registry properties:
that a version may be scheduled in advance, and that a later anchoring may not
be given a validity start at or before one already scheduled. It does not take
effect during a demonstration run.
